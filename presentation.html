PROJECT FOUNDATION

Problem Statement & Key Objectives

Replacing cumbersome manual billing with an automated, auditable telecom calculation architecture

Bottlenecks in Legacy Telecom Operations

1. Error-Prone Pulse Calculations

Manual rounding of call durations leads to recurring customer disputes and compliance fines.

2. Free Quota Tracking Failures

Deducting monthly plan free-minutes against diverse call classifications requires automated algorithmic checks.

3. Statutory GST & Surcharge Overhead

Complex Indian telecom taxation (18% GST split into CGST 9% + SGST 9%) plus late fee rules.

4. Opaque Invoicing

Lack of clear itemized CDR logs causes bill-shock and customer churn.

Our Engineered Solutions & Objectives

1. Automated Telecom Rating Engine

Duration ceiling to 60s pulses, free quota deductions, and tier-specific tariff rating.

2. Real-Time Prefix Classifier

Instant parsing of destination numbers into LOCAL, national STD (022/080), or ISD international.

3. Authentic Airtel/Jio Tax Invoice (Centerpiece)

Itemized billing breakdown, complete CDR itemization table, status stamps, and instant OpenPDF export.

4. Dual Desktop & Cloud Access

Enterprise offline JavaFX app for on-premise telecom servers and responsive Vercel web portal.

SYSTEM SHOWCASE — MODULE 2

Call Detail Records (CDR) & Prefix Engine

Real-time destination number classification, pulse conversion, and bulk log ingestion

ISD International Classification

Numbers prefixed with '+' or '00' (excluding +91) automatically trigger ISD rating (e.g. +14155552671, +442079460912).

STD National Trunk Detection

Domestic trunk codes beginning with '0' (022 Mumbai, 080 Bangalore, 011 Delhi) are classified as STD long distance.

Local Circle Calls

Standard 10-digit mobile lines or +91 prefixes are routed to the subscriber's local tariff tier.

Bulk CSV Ingestion

Supports drag-and-drop batch upload of switch CDR files for automated multi-call processing.

CORE BUSINESS LOGIC — MODULE 3

Automated Telecom Billing Engine

Mathematical rating formulas, free allowance prioritisation, and statutory tax compliance

Rating Engine Pipeline

Step 1: 60-Second Pulse Ceiling

mins = Math.ceil(durationSeconds / 60.0);

Step 2: Free Minutes Quota Deduction

chargeableLocal = Math.max(0, localMins - plan.freeMinutes);

Step 3: Call Charges Aggregation

callsTotal = (chargeableLocal * rLocal) + (stdMins * rStd) + (isdMins * rIsd);

Step 4: Statutory GST & Surcharges

subtotal = callsTotal + rental; gst = subtotal * 0.18 (9% CGST + 9% SGST);

Step 5: Final Payable Computation

payable = Math.max(0, subtotal + gst + lateFee - discount);
